Worship Lyrics Study

A data study of 352 worship songs, 1900 to 2025

Not true for the century. Rising this decade.

Sit through a modern worship set and you will hear a lot of "I" and "me." Has worship drifted from God toward us? We counted every word in the most-used church songs of each decade since 1900.

The short answer: across the century, no. Today's songs say "I" about as often as the gospel songs of 1900 to 1969. But since 2000 it has climbed every decade, and songs from the 2020s now match the 1900s peak.

A closer look

The newest songs are climbing

Look only at songs written since 2000 and the trend runs one way. Each decade says "I" more than the one before.

The long view

The 1970s and 80s were the outlier

Gospel songs from the early 1900s said "I" as often as today's songs. The praise choruses of the 1970s and 80s pulled it down. Since 1990 it has climbed back.
